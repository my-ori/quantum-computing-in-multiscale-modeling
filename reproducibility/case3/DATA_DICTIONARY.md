# Case III data dictionary

All CSV files have a header row and retain full floating-point precision unless explicitly described as a published snapshot. Times are in ps, displacements in nm, and velocities in nm/ps. Atom indices in the validation and sensitivity files are **zero based** (0, 1, 2). The Figure 6 export uses the historical **one-based** `atom` column (1, 2, 3).

## Full five-level comparison

`results/case3_validation_trajectories.csv` has 453 rows and `time_ps`, `atom_index`, and two columns for each solution level: `*_displacement_nm` and `*_reconstructed_velocity_backward_nm_per_ps`. Prefixes are `full_lj`, `quadratic_nonlinear`, `continuous_k2_carleman`, `backward_euler_k2_carleman`, and `vqls`.

`results/case3_reference_states.npz` contains:

| Array | Shape | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| `time_ps` | 151 | Comparison sample times |
| `A_C`, `M_C` | 42 × 42 | Retained generator and backward-Euler matrix |
| `initial_z`, `initial_Y` | 6; 42 | Initial physical and lifted states |
| `full_lj_native_q_v` | 151 × 6 | DOP853 full Lennard–Jones state `[q0,q1,q2,v0,v1,v2]` |
| `quadratic_nonlinear_native_q_v` | 151 × 6 | DOP853 quadratic-force state in the same order |
| `continuous_k2_lifted_state` | 151 × 42 | Continuous Carleman state `[z;Y2]`; Y2 is propagated independently |
| `backward_euler_k2_lifted_state` | 151 × 42 | Unprojected classical backward-Euler state |
| `vqls_displacement_nm` | 151 × 3 | Unmodified retained VQLS displacements |

`case3_error_decomposition_summary.csv` gives maximum, RMS, and mean absolute displacement and reconstructed-velocity differences for all five comparisons. `case3_error_decomposition_by_time.csv` has 755 rows (five comparisons × 151 times), with maximum and RMS differences across the three atoms at each time. `table7.csv` selects the four metrics shown in the manuscript; `reproduced/table7.tex` is generated by the builder and formats them to three significant figures.

## Consistency and projection

`results/case3_consistency_states.npz` contains the 151 sample times, initial 42-component state, and four full 151 × 42 state histories: `continuous_k2_lifted_state`, `unprojected_be_lifted_state`, `relifted_be_preprojection_state`, and `relifted_be_postprojection_state`. It also contains the 151 × 6 `full_lj_native_q_v` reference for sensitivity comparisons and the separate 42-component `retained_vqls_terminal_state` with scalar `retained_vqls_terminal_time_ps = 0.4`.

For each lifted row, entries 0–5 are z, and entries 6–41 are Y2 in NumPy Kronecker ordering: `Y2.reshape(6,6)[i,j]` corresponds to the lifted variable for z[i]z[j]. The lift need not equal the instantaneous product except immediately after explicit projection. Mixed physical units are retained exactly as in the manuscript's numerical state convention; the consistency norm is the stated Euclidean norm of these stored components.

`case3_carleman_consistency_by_time.csv` has 151 rows of absolute and normalized defects for continuous, unprojected, pre-projection, and post-projection states. Its additional velocity-difference field is explicitly labeled `native_velocity`. `case3_carleman_consistency_summary.csv` contains the four classical summaries plus the auxiliary terminal-only VQLS record. `results/table8.csv` and the generated `reproduced/table8.tex` include only the four classical rows.

`case3_projection_trajectories.csv` has 453 rows. Each of the prefixes `full_lj`, `continuous_k2`, `unprojected_be`, and `relifted_be` has displacement and **native velocity** columns. `case3_projection_sensitivity_summary.csv` gives maximum, RMS, and terminal-maximum errors for re-lifted versus unprojected BE, unprojected BE versus full Lennard–Jones, and re-lifted BE versus full Lennard–Jones. Its native-velocity metrics must not be substituted for Table 7's reconstructed-velocity metrics.

## Figure exports

These exports are generated in `reproduced/` (or the selected output directory) and are not additional archived files in `results/`.

`figure6_series.csv` exports the VQLS and classical backward-Euler comparison with explicit units and backward-reconstruction labels. The values reproduce the previously public series to floating-point roundoff. `figure7_cost_full_precision.csv` contains the first 150 retained VQLS cost records without the rounding of the historical public CSV. The summary JSON contains first, final, mean, and maximum costs.
